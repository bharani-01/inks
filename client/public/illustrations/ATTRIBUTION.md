# Illustration credits

The 3D printer hero (`clay-printer.png`) is a project-provided asset (also at
`public/images/clay_printer_hero.png`). It is the only bitmap illustration in the app.

- `clay-printer.png` — project asset (clay 3D printer hero, used on the Print Hub)

All other in-app iconography and representations (file-type badges, the document-upload
scan animation, section icons, etc.) are rendered as inline SVG or
[lucide](https://lucide.dev) icons (ISC License) — no emoji or third-party image assets.
